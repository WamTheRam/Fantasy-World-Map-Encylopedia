## Nature
Ember drakes are the size of large dogs, with scales that glow faintly when they are warm. They nest on the warm ledges of the Ember Peaks and are curious about anything shiny.

## Relations with the clans
The Karn forge-guilds have a working truce with the drakes: scrap metal left at certain ledges is taken, and no smith is bothered. Nobody who has broken the truce is willing to discuss it.
