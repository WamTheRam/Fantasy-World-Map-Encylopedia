Its society revolves around the idea of the “flock.” Mortals are well-fed, safe, entertained, and protected, but they can never become nobles and have little meaningful control over their country's politics. The ruling class genuinely cares for them, but that affection is possessive and paternalistic: mortals are treated as something precious and fragile that must be kept within carefully defined boundaries.

The empire is governed by the three Great Bodies: [[the-sun]], [[the-moon]], and [[the-revelry]].
