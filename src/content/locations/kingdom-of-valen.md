## The land
Valen is a country of long roads and short tempers. Its wheat fields feed three kingdoms, its river towns keep the ledgers, and almost everything of importance happens within a day's ride of a shrine.

## The crown
The throne has passed through [[house-valen]] since [[the-founding|the Founding]]. The present monarch, Queen Elara II, rules from Aurelia under the valen-monarchy, a system in which the crown owns the roads and the road-wardens own the crown's conscience.

## Faith
Most Valenites keep the oaths of Veyr and are wary of anyone who won't. Harvest festivals honour Solara, and sailors on the Eastern Coast pray to Maren when no one is listening.

## Neighbours
- **Karn Holds**, to the north-east: old rivals, uneasy partners, and the other side of the northern-border-dispute.
- **Republic of Solmere**, across the water: neutral, wealthy, and always happy to lend at a fair rate.
