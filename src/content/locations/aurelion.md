Its society is highly hierarchical and formal, with chivalry and ambition existing side by side. Rather than relying primarily on conquest, Aurelion expands through marriages, adoptions, treaties, and political maneuvering.

The empire is ruled by [[house-epityclise]], whose philosophy is essentially:

“The Title Makes the Man.”

Aurelion is known for white-stone fortresses, gilded architecture, noble courts, military academies, and prestigious mercenary-knights. House Epityclise in particular believes it was destined to unify Argoyll and requires its heirs to personally prove themselves through military victory before inheriting the throne.

At its core, Aurelion values earning honor, protecting one's name, fulfilling one's obligations, and winning with dignity, but its polished chivalry hides a highly calculating political culture.
