## A fortress on a pass
Greywatch is the anvil on which Valen's northern policy is hammered. Its walls have been rebuilt four times and breached never; its archives record the [[siege-of-greywatch|forty-day siege]] with a precision that suggests nobody has quite forgiven it.

## The Watch
The fortress is home to the Order of the Grey Watch, commanded by Warden Isolde Marsh. Recruits swear their oaths on the wall itself, in the name of Veyr, and are then given a very large amount of stone to move.
