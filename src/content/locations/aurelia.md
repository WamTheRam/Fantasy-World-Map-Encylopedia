## Overview
Aurelia has been the capital of kingdom-of-valen since [[the-founding|the Founding]]. It is home to House Valen and the seat of Queen Elara II, and its white-stone terraces climb a low hill above the river.

## The Terraces
Each terrace is a district with its own guild, its own market day and its own opinion of the others. The lowest, by the river, is the loudest; the highest holds the royal court and the quietest gardens in the kingdom.

## Faith
The city keeps the oaths of Veyr. Every new stall, contract and marriage is witnessed at the small roadside shrine by the eastern gate, and the ceremony has never once been skipped.

> "A road that leads to Aurelia is a road that has kept its word." Valen proverb
