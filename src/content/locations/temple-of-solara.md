## The sun court
The temple is a terraced court open to the sky, built so that the dawn on the solstices fills its central pool with gold. Pilgrims arrive the night before and sleep on the steps.

## Practice
Sister Liora leads the morning rites, and the Children of the Dawn tend the grounds. Novices are taught the rudiments of sun-glass-magic: how to catch a sunrise in a lens and carry it indoors.
