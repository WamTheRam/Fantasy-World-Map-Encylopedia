## The line
House Valen has held the throne of Valen since [[the-founding|the Founding]], through nineteen monarchs, two regencies and one very awkward succession. Its motto is a road oath: *what I walk, I keep*.

## Standing
The house is bound to Veyr by an old oath, renewed at each coronation, most recently at the [[coronation-of-elara-ii|coronation of Elara II]]. The oath forbids the house from closing a road to any honest traveller, which has shaped Valen's politics more than any army.
