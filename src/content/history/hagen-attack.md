## Background
Hagen the Red had been exiled from the northern marches and gathered exiles, raiders and the desperate into what became Hagen's Army. He tried Greywatch first, and failed.

## The march
After the siege, Hagen marched south through the pass toward the plains. Queen Elara II, then a junior officer, was among the defenders who met him at Hagen's Field.

## Outcome
The army was broken in a single afternoon. Hagen fell on the field, and the name of the place has been his memorial ever since.
