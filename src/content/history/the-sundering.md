## The night
On a still night in Year 412, fire fell from the sky over the Deepwater Vale. The ground opened, lakes boiled, and the old kingdoms of the north-east lost their capitals in a single hour.

## Aftermath
The land recovered slowly. The [[founding-of-the-council-of-clans|clans of Karn]] swore to govern together, and in Valen, the [[the-oath-of-veyr|Oath of Veyr]] was taken to make the roads safe. The broken spire on the shore of the Vale is all that remains of what stood there.
