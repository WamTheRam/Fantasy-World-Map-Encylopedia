## Domain
Veyr is the god of oaths, roads and safe passage. He is not a god of law, exactly; he is the witness who is always present when a promise is made.

## Worship
His shrines are simple: a stone, a lamp and a bowl for coins. Travellers leave a word at wanderers-cairn, and merchants swear to their contracts on the road rather than in a hall.

## Symbols
A milestone, an open hand, and a lamp with a single flame.
