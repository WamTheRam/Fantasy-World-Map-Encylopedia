## Structure
Every clan of the Karn Holds sends a delegate, and no decision is taken until every delegate has spoken. Clan-Master Durgan Vael presides, which mostly means deciding when everyone has finished.

## Beliefs
The Council swears by Durok and takes oaths on an anvil. It was formed after [[the-sundering|the Sundering]], and its founding charter is carved into the wall of the hall at Kharrow.
