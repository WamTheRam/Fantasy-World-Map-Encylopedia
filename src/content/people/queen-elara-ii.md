## Life
Elara II was a young officer of the northern march when Hagen the Red came south, and she fought at Hagen's Field. It is said that she has never since ridden a road without checking its milestones.

She was crowned decades later, in Aurelia, and is known for a stubborn programme of road-building, patient dealings with Karn, and a habit of arriving unannounced at provincial courts.

## Beliefs
She keeps the oaths of Veyr more strictly than the law requires, and is said to have never broken one, though she has occasionally been very careful about how she phrased them.
